Which WLTP class?

The WLTP test (UN GTR 15) uses a different drive cycle depending on the vehicle's power-to-mass ratio (PMR = rated power ÷ kerb mass, in W/kg) and its maximum speed. Each cycle lasts 1,800 s (30 minutes) and has Low, Medium, High and Extra High phases.

  • 3a vs 3b: the same Low and Extra High phases; the Medium and High phases differ slightly.
  • Class 2 has lower maximum speeds in every phase (51.4, 74.7, 85.2 and 123.1 km/h, against 56.5, 76.6, 97.4 and 131.3 km/h for Class 3).
  • Mass: GTR 15 uses kerb mass; EU rules use mass in running order (kerb mass + 75 kg), which gives a slightly lower PMR.
  • Downscaling: the official test can slow the cycle down for vehicles near a class boundary or with very low power. This calculator always runs the full cycle and reports any time the vehicle cannot keep up.

Sources: UN GTR No. 15 (WLTP); DieselNet, “Worldwide Harmonized Light Vehicles Test Cycle (WLTC)”. Cycle data from the European Commission JRC wltp reference implementation.
